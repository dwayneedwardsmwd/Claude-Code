Mud Weight Conversions

Convert between different mud weight units.

Quick Reference

  • Fresh Water: 8.33 PPG = 1.0 SG
  • Sea Water: 8.55 PPG = 1.03 SG
  • Pressure Gradient: PPG × 0.052 = PSI/ft
  • Hydrostatic: PPG × 0.052 × TVD = PSI
